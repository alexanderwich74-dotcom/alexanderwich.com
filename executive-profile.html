Executive Profile

Practical leadership in complex operational environments.

My career has taught me that strong operations depend on more than technical knowledge. They depend on clear priorities, capable people, reliable systems and the willingness to take responsibility when decisions need to be made.

Understanding the whole operation.

My experience across facilities, technical operations, IT and hospitality has taught me to look at an operation as a connected system. Buildings, technology, people, suppliers and business priorities all affect one another.

Understanding those connections helps me identify what matters, where risks sit and how operational decisions will affect the wider business.

Responsibility before hierarchy.

For me, responsibility means being present when decisions matter, creating clear priorities and supporting the people who have to deliver them. Titles and hierarchy are less important than ownership, judgement and follow-through.

Whether dealing with technical operations, projects, suppliers, hotel openings or operational incidents, I believe in involving the right expertise, making practical decisions and remaining accountable for the outcome.

Building capability that remains.

A strong operation should not depend on one individual. It should have clear responsibilities, useful documentation, reliable systems, accountable suppliers and people who understand how to act when conditions change.

That is the kind of capability I aim to leave behind: practical structures that support the team, improve reliability and help the organisation continue to perform after the immediate project or challenge has passed.

What I Believe

The principles behind how I work.

Take Responsibility

Own the decision, follow through and remain accountable.

Create Clarity

People perform better when priorities, expectations and responsibilities are understood.

Trust Capable People

Give people responsibility, support them when needed and allow expertise to work.

Think Long Term

Solve today’s problem without creating tomorrow’s.

“To leave every organisation stronger than I found it.”

— Alexander Wich
